# Mi Band Bridge 1.0.0

Unofficial. Not made by or connected with Xiaomi.

The first public release: Mi Band Bridge and the Mi Band App Editor 1.0.0 for a
Mac with Apple silicon and for Windows, Mi Band Bridge 2.4.0 for Android, and
the sample band app BandArrows 1.0.4.

## What is in it

- **Mi Band Bridge** holds a Xiaomi Smart Band 7 over Bluetooth, with no phone,
  and runs actions when you press a button on the band: a keystroke or a media
  key, a shell command or a script, a shortcut from Shortcuts.app on a Mac, or a
  web request. It also installs and removes band apps, and arranges the band's
  app menu and shortcuts.
- **Mi Band App Editor** makes a band app of buttons, with no code, and installs
  it through Mi Band Bridge.
- **Mi Band Bridge for Android** runs a Key Mapper key map when you press a
  button on the band, while Gadgetbridge holds the band.
- **BandArrows** is a band app with an up and a down button, ready to install.

## Before you install

- You need your band's auth key. The README's "Get the band's auth key" says
  how to get it.
- The Mac apps are signed and notarized by Apple.
- The Windows installers are not signed. Windows SmartScreen stops each one on
  its first run: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Mi Band Bridge for Android needs Gadgetbridge and Key Mapper.

## Limits

- Mi Band Bridge holds one band at a time, and the band takes one host at a
  time: a Mac, a Windows PC or a phone.
- The Mac apps need Apple silicon.
- Windows has no Shortcut action, because Shortcuts is a Mac app.
- Mi Band Bridge does not sync fitness data.
